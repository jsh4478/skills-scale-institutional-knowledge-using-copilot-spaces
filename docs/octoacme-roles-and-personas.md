# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads define and oversee quality strategies, manage testing phases, and ensure all deliverables meet established quality standards. They work closely with development and project teams to identify quality risks and drive continuous improvement.

### Responsibilities
- Define QA strategies and testing approaches aligned with project goals
- Oversee all testing phases (unit, integration, system, user acceptance testing)
- Identify quality risks and recommend mitigation strategies
- Ensure deliverables meet quality standards and acceptance criteria
- Collaborate with developers on test case design and coverage
- Report quality metrics and test results to stakeholders
- Advocate for quality improvements and best practices

### Goals
- Deliver high-quality, reliable software
- Minimize defects reaching production
- Reduce testing cycle time through efficient QA processes
- Build confidence in product quality across the organization

### Typical Communication
- Test planning sessions and progress reviews with Execution Lead
- Quality metrics and defect reports to Program Manager
- Test case documentation and quality dashboards
- Coordination with developers on bug resolution

### Key Interactions
- **Works with Execution Lead** on test scheduling and resource allocation
- **Collaborates with Developers** on test automation and quality requirements
- **Reports to Program Manager** on quality status and readiness gates
- **Supports Product Manager** on quality acceptance criteria

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects define technical direction, review design proposals, and ensure technical feasibility of requirements. They identify technical risks, guide architectural decisions, and ensure alignment with organizational technology standards and scalability goals.

### Responsibilities
- Define technical direction and architectural standards
- Review and validate design proposals for feasibility and alignment
- Identify technical risks and propose mitigation strategies
- Ensure technical feasibility of business requirements
- Guide technology choices and evaluate trade-offs
- Support knowledge sharing and technical mentorship
- Monitor emerging technologies and industry trends

### Goals
- Ensure scalable, maintainable technical solutions
- Reduce technical debt and future rework
- Align projects with organizational technology standards
- Enable team growth through technical guidance

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Design documentation and technical specifications
- Risk assessments and mitigation plans
- Architecture decision records (ADRs)

### Key Interactions
- **Collaborates with Product Manager** on requirement validation and feasibility
- **Advises Execution Lead** on technical dependencies and risks
- **Mentors Developers** on architectural patterns and best practices
- **Partners with Technical team leads** on technical strategy

---

## Stakeholder Manager / Communications Lead

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers own all internal and external stakeholder communications, manage expectations, address concerns, and maintain comprehensive communication plans. They ensure alignment and transparency across all project participants and stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Develop and maintain stakeholder communication plans
- Identify stakeholders and assess communication needs
- Manage expectations through regular, transparent updates
- Escalate concerns and issues to appropriate decision-makers
- Facilitate communication across teams and departments
- Gather stakeholder feedback and concerns
- Maintain communication records and documentation

### Goals
- Ensure stakeholder alignment and satisfaction
- Reduce surprises and manage expectations proactively
- Build trust and transparency across the project
- Facilitate effective collaboration among diverse stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Status updates and newsletters for stakeholder groups
- Stakeholder feedback sessions and engagement activities
- Escalation reports and concern logs
- Communication plans and stakeholder maps

### Key Interactions
- **Reports to Program Manager** on stakeholder health and engagement
- **Coordinates with all roles** on communication needs and updates
- **Partners with Product Manager** on roadmap and feature communication
- **Supports Project Manager** on status reporting and escalations

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers coordinate all release activities, manage release schedules, own deployment coordination, and ensure release readiness. They serve as the central coordinator for moving code from development through production.

### Responsibilities
- Plan and coordinate release schedules and timelines
- Manage release documentation and release notes
- Coordinate deployment activities across environments
- Verify release readiness and manage go/no-go decisions
- Communicate release status to stakeholders
- Coordinate rollback procedures and contingency plans
- Track release metrics and post-release performance

### Goals
- Execute reliable, on-time releases
- Minimize deployment risks and issues
- Ensure smooth transition to production
- Improve release velocity and predictability

### Typical Communication
- Release plans and deployment schedules
- Release status updates and go/no-go assessments
- Release notes and deployment documentation
- Post-release review and retrospectives

### Key Interactions
- **Partners with Execution Lead** on release readiness and resource coordination
- **Coordinates with QA Lead** on testing completion and quality gates
- **Works with Technical team** on deployment procedures and rollback plans
- **Reports to Program Manager** on release status and outcomes

---

## Risk Manager / Compliance Officer

### Role Summary
Risk Managers focus specifically on risk assessment, compliance verification, audit support, and mitigation strategy oversight. They ensure projects identify, track, and address risks proactively while maintaining compliance with organizational and regulatory requirements.

### Responsibilities
- Conduct risk assessments and maintain risk registers
- Identify and evaluate potential risks and impacts
- Define and track risk mitigation strategies
- Monitor compliance with organizational policies and standards
- Support audit activities and provide compliance documentation
- Escalate critical risks and compliance issues
- Report risk status to leadership and stakeholders
- Recommend process improvements to reduce risk

### Goals
- Proactively identify and mitigate project risks
- Ensure compliance with all applicable standards
- Reduce project disruptions and unforeseen issues
- Support organizational risk management objectives

### Typical Communication
- Risk registers and risk assessments
- Compliance reports and audit documentation
- Risk escalation notices and mitigation updates
- Risk review meetings and status reports

### Key Interactions
- **Works with all roles** to identify and track risks
- **Reports to Program Manager** on risk status and escalations
- **Partners with Project Manager** on risk monitoring and mitigation
- **Supports Execution Lead** on technical and operational risk management

---

## Subject Matter Expert (SME)

### Role Summary
Subject Matter Experts provide deep domain expertise, validate requirements against business practices, support training initiatives, and ensure alignment with organizational and industry standards. They bridge the gap between business requirements and technical implementation.

### Responsibilities
- Provide expert guidance on domain-specific topics and best practices
- Validate requirements and solutions against business knowledge
- Support training and knowledge transfer activities
- Advise on compliance with industry standards and regulations
- Mentor team members on domain-specific practices
- Identify gaps between proposed solutions and business needs
- Support user acceptance testing and validation

### Goals
- Ensure solutions align with business reality and best practices
- Reduce rework due to domain misalignment
- Build organizational knowledge and capability
- Support successful user adoption

### Typical Communication
- Subject matter expertise documentation and guidelines
- Requirements validation and feasibility assessments
- Training materials and knowledge transfer sessions
- Best practice recommendations and process guidance

### Key Interactions
- **Advises Product Manager** on requirements and business alignment
- **Supports Execution Lead** on implementation decisions
- **Collaborates with Developers** on technical implementation details
- **Supports Stakeholder Manager** with educational content and training
